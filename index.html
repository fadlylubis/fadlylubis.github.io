Fadly Lubis

Data Engineer at Park Place Technologies

fadlylubis.streamlit.app

I’m a Data Engineer at Park Place Technologies. I’m leading the move of our legacy pipelines from Azure Data Factory & Synapse Pipelines to Microsoft Fabric Data Factory. I also built monitoring that covers every orchestration engine the company runs, and data contracts with Great Expectations.

Before that I spent three years as a Machine Learning Engineer at Service Express. There I built PartsRX, an NLP model that predicts the hardware parts a customer issue needs, and OnDeck, a demand planning tool that forecasts parts demand from 30 years of contract & demand history using XGBoost, a custom uncertainty algorithm and Monte Carlo stress testing. I also built a vector search engine over millions of records that answers in under 50 ms, and ran the Airflow, Prefect and MLflow platforms on Kubernetes.

I started out in BI, data analysis and e-commerce, so I have worked on every step from raw data to a deployed model. I hold an M.S. in Information System Engineering from the University of Michigan–Dearborn and a B.S. in Information Systems Management from the University of Maryland Global Campus.
